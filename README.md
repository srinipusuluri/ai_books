## 💡 The Idea Behind This Series

This series is built for **high school students** who want to step into the world of AI, starting *now*, not after college.

| | |
|:--|:--|
| 🌱 **Start early** | Curiosity at 15 becomes a superpower at 25. |
| 🧩 **Plain language** | Big ideas explained simply, with no heavy math or buzzwords required. |
| 🛠️ **Learn by doing** | Real tools, real examples, real confidence. |
| 🦁 **Fearless mindset** | You don't need to be an expert to begin. You just need to begin. |
| 🌍 **Future-ready** | Understand the technology that will shape your career and your world. |

Each book picks one doorway into AI, so you can go from *"What is AI?"* to *"I can build with this!"* one step at a time.
